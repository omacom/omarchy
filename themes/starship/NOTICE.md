# Additional Starship wallpaper credits

The 28 additional wallpapers listed in [wallpapers.json](wallpapers.json) comprise 16 photographs supplied from SpaceX’s official X posts and all 12 backgrounds from [Steve Derico’s Space theme](https://github.com/stevederico/omarchy-space-theme), pinned to commit `d8830116ce13b55df22815be21ff131be5ff9f06`.

The supplied 16 photographs retain their original dimensions. The Space theme describes its 6K variants as Topaz High Fidelity V2 upscales; these variants are retained without further AI processing. Some shots appear in both collections at different resolutions. That repository lists the original source of `7-starship.jpg` as unknown; the manifest preserves that uncertainty and no ownership is claimed.

The manifest records source URLs, dimensions, original and distributed SHA-256 checksums, and processing details. Images are encoded as sRGB WebP without colour filters. The `5-` filename prefix appends this collection after the existing four wallpapers, preserving `1-starbase.webp` as the fresh-install default.

The photographs retain their original rights and are excluded from Omarchy’s MIT licence. See [SpaceX’s media policy](https://www.spacex.com/trademark).
