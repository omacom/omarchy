# Photography credits

The original 16 wallpaper photographs are © SpaceX, supplied from its official X posts for this independent, non-commercial community theme. The additional 12 backgrounds come from Steve Derico’s [Space theme](https://github.com/stevederico/omarchy-space-theme), introduced in [omarchy-site PR #436](https://github.com/omacom/omarchy-site/pull/436). That repository credits its photographs to SpaceX, documents its 6K upscales, and lists the original source of `7-starship.jpg` as unknown. Starship retains that source uncertainty; no ownership of these images is claimed.

The source attachment URLs, original filenames, original dimensions, and SHA-256 checksums are recorded in [wallpapers.json](wallpapers.json). The distributed wallpapers are sRGB WebP encodings at the original dimensions. The photographs remain copyright SpaceX and are not covered by the theme's MIT licence.

SpaceX publishes its media usage policy at [spacex.com/trademark](https://www.spacex.com/trademark). This theme is not endorsed by SpaceX or NASA.
